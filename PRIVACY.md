# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 21, 2026**

AnuLaya ("the App") is developed by AnuLaya. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you create an account using Google Sign-In or email, we collect:
- Email address
- Display name
- Profile photo (Google Sign-In only)

This information is used to identify your compositions, playlists, and practice sessions.

### User-Generated Content
- Compositions you create (title, author, notation, settings)
- Playlists you create or follow
- Practice session data (scores, timing, cycle-by-cycle accuracy)

### Audio Recordings
When you use Practice mode, the app accesses your device microphone to provide real-time feedback. Practice sessions may be recorded and saved to your account. You can review, share, or delete any recording from the Practice tab at any time.

### Usage Data
We collect basic usage data to improve the app experience. This data is not shared with third parties.

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide and maintain the App's functionality
- To save and sync your compositions, playlists, and practice sessions across devices
- To improve practice scoring, stroke recognition, and related AI/ML models using anonymized audio and session data
- To display your compositions and playlists to other users (when published or shared)

## Data Storage

Your data is stored using Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, provided by Google. Data is stored on servers in the United States. For more information, see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Audio recordings are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage.

Display preferences (script, default tempo) are stored locally on your device.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal data with third parties. Audio recordings and practice session data may be used to train and improve AnuLaya's AI and machine learning models for practice scoring, stroke classification, and rhythm analysis. This data is used in anonymized form and is not shared externally for purposes unrelated to the App.

## Your Choices

- **Publishing:** Compositions and playlists are private by default. You choose when to publish or share them.
- **Practice recordings:** You can delete any practice session or audio recording at any time from the Practice tab.
- **Account deletion:** You can delete your account and all associated data from the Settings screen.
- **Anonymous use:** You can browse and play compositions without creating an account.

## Children's Privacy

AnuLaya does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App is designed for general audiences interested in learning tabla.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at [https://github.com/ashukuls/tabla/issues](https://github.com/ashukuls/tabla/issues).
